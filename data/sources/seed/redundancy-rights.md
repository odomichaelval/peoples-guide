If you are made redundant you might be entitled to redundancy pay, a notice period, a consultation with your employer, the option to move into a different job, and time off to find a new job.

You are normally entitled to statutory redundancy pay if you are an employee and have worked for your employer for two years or more. You should get any holiday pay you are owed and any notice pay.

If you think your redundancy was unfair, you can get free advice from Acas or Citizens Advice. There are strict time limits for making a claim to an employment tribunal, so seek advice quickly.
