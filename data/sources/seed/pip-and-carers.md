Personal Independence Payment (PIP) can help with extra living costs if you have a long-term physical or mental health condition or disability, and difficulty doing certain everyday tasks or getting around because of it. You can get PIP even if you are working, have savings or are getting most other benefits. It is not means-tested. You must normally be 16 or over and under State Pension age to make a new claim.

Carer's Allowance is a payment if you care for someone for at least 35 hours a week and they get certain benefits (such as PIP daily living component). Your earnings must be below a set limit. Carer's Allowance is at https://www.gov.uk/carers-allowance.
