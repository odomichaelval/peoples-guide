Sheffield City Council runs local schemes to help residents in financial crisis with essentials such as food, energy bills and household items. These schemes (for example local welfare assistance and any Household Support Fund-type support) change over time and have their own eligibility rules, so check the council website for what is currently open. Search sheffield.gov.uk for "cost of living" or "help with money".

Support is usually aimed at people on a low income, people claiming benefits, and households facing an unexpected crisis.

Food banks in Sheffield can provide emergency food parcels. You usually need a referral from an agency such as Citizens Advice, a GP, a school, or the council. Trussell can help you find your nearest food bank.
