Universal Credit is a monthly payment to help with living costs. You may be able to get it if you are on a low income, out of work, or cannot work.

Who can usually apply: you must live in the UK, be aged 18 or over (there are some exceptions for 16 and 17 year olds), be under State Pension age (or your partner is), and have £16,000 or less in money, savings and investments. If you live with a partner you make a joint claim.

Universal Credit can include extra amounts for housing costs (help paying rent), children, childcare, disability or health conditions that limit work, and caring for someone.

If you have recently lost your job, you can apply straight away. Your final pay may affect your first payment.

How to claim: apply online on GOV.UK. You need an email address, phone number, bank account details, housing details such as your tenancy agreement and rent, and details of income and savings. You will need to verify your identity. The first payment usually arrives about five weeks after you apply.

If you cannot manage until your first payment you can ask for an advance payment, which you pay back from later payments.
