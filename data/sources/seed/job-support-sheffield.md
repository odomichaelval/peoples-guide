Jobcentre Plus work coaches help people find work, including job search support, CV help, and access to training. If you claim Universal Credit or New Style JSA you will be given a work coach.

You can search for jobs using the government's Find a Job service on GOV.UK.

Sheffield City Council supports local employment and skills programmes that offer free help with job searching, training, apprenticeships and adult learning. Search sheffield.gov.uk for "employment and skills" or "adult learning" to see current programmes.
