If you are homeless or might become homeless in the next 56 days, the council has a legal duty to help. Contact Sheffield City Council's housing options/homelessness service (sheffield.gov.uk, search "homeless"). If you have nowhere safe to sleep tonight, contact the council straight away, including out of hours.

The council must assess your situation and agree a personal housing plan with you. If you are in priority need (for example you have children, are pregnant, or are vulnerable), the council may have to provide emergency accommodation.

If you have received an eviction notice (such as a section 8 notice) from a private landlord, get advice immediately. The notice may not be valid. Shelter offers free expert housing advice online and via its helpline. Citizens Advice can also help.

You should not leave your home just because you have received a notice. Only a court and bailiffs can legally evict you.
