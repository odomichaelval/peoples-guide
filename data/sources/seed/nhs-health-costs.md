In England you may get free NHS prescriptions, free dental treatment, free eye tests, and help with travel costs to hospital if you are on a low income or get certain benefits, such as Universal Credit (within earnings limits), income-based Jobseeker's Allowance, or Pension Credit Guarantee Credit.

If you do not get those benefits but are on a low income, you can apply to the NHS Low Income Scheme (form HC1) for full or partial help.

For urgent medical help that is not an emergency, use NHS 111 online or call 111. For mental health crisis support, you can also call NHS 111 and choose the mental health option.
