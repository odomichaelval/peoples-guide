Council Tax Support (also called Council Tax Reduction) can lower your council tax bill if you are on a low income or claim benefits. In Sheffield it is run by Sheffield City Council, which sets its own scheme rules for working-age residents. Pension-age rules are set nationally.

You could get support whether you own your home, rent, are unemployed or working. What you get depends on your income and savings, your circumstances (for example children or a disability), and who else lives with you.

Apply to Sheffield City Council. You can apply online through the council website (sheffield.gov.uk, search "Council Tax Support"). If you have just claimed Universal Credit, you still need to apply for Council Tax Support separately - it is not automatic.

Discounts are separate from Council Tax Support. For example, if you live alone you may get a 25% single person discount. Some people are disregarded for council tax, such as full-time students.

If you are falling behind with council tax, contact the council as early as possible to arrange a payment plan.
