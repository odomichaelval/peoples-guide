Citizens Advice offers free, confidential, independent advice on benefits, work, debt and money, housing, and consumer problems. Citizens Advice Sheffield can help you check which benefits you are entitled to and help you fill in applications. You can get advice online, by phone, or in person.

If you have debts, free debt advice charities such as StepChange can help you make a plan, deal with creditors, and look at debt solutions. MoneyHelper (a government-backed service) gives free guidance on money and pensions.

Priority debts - like rent, council tax and energy - can have the most serious consequences if not paid, so deal with them first.

If you are struggling to cope, Samaritans are available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, free on 116 123. In an emergency always call 999.
