Most working-age people now get help with rent through the housing element of Universal Credit rather than Housing Benefit. You can usually only make a new claim for Housing Benefit if you have reached State Pension age, or live in supported, sheltered or temporary housing.

If your Universal Credit housing element or Housing Benefit does not cover all your rent, you can apply to Sheffield City Council for a Discretionary Housing Payment (DHP). DHPs are extra short-term payments from the council to help with rent shortfalls, rent in advance, or a deposit when moving. You must be getting Housing Benefit or the housing element of Universal Credit to apply. Apply through the council website (sheffield.gov.uk, search "Discretionary Housing Payment").

If you are behind with rent, speak to your landlord and get advice early. Your landlord must follow a legal process to evict you.
