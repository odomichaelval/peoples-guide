Your child may be able to get free school meals if you get certain benefits, such as Universal Credit (with household earnings below a set threshold), Income Support, income-based Jobseeker's Allowance, income-related Employment and Support Allowance, or the guarantee element of Pension Credit.

All children in reception, year 1 and year 2 at state schools in England get free school meals regardless of income.

Apply through your local council - in Sheffield, through Sheffield City Council. Children who qualify for free school meals may also be able to get help with holiday activities and food in the school holidays.
