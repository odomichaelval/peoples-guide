Pension Credit gives you extra money to help with living costs if you are over State Pension age and on a low income. It can also help with housing costs.

Getting Pension Credit can also unlock other help, such as Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction, the Warm Home Discount, a free TV licence if you are 75 or over, and help with NHS dental treatment and glasses.

You can apply online, by phone, or by post. You can apply up to 4 months before you reach State Pension age.
