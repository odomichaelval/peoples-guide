If you are waiting for your first Universal Credit payment and need money now, you can request an advance payment. You can ask for it in your online journal, by calling the Universal Credit helpline, or through your work coach at the Jobcentre. The advance is a loan that is paid back out of future Universal Credit payments.

If you have been getting Universal Credit for a while, you may be able to get a Budgeting Advance to help with emergency household costs such as replacing a broken cooker, getting a job, or rent in advance for a new home.

You can also ask for your rent to be paid directly to your landlord (an Alternative Payment Arrangement) if you are struggling to manage your payments or are in rent arrears.
