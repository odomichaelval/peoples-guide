Students in higher education can apply for a Tuition Fee Loan and a Maintenance Loan for living costs through Student Finance England. You may also get extra help if you have children, an adult dependant, or a disability (Disabled Students' Allowance).

Universities in Sheffield (the University of Sheffield and Sheffield Hallam University) run their own hardship or financial support funds for students in financial difficulty. Contact your university's student support or money advice team.

Most full-time students cannot claim Universal Credit, but there are exceptions, for example if you are responsible for a child, or you have a disability and get certain benefits.

Full-time students are usually disregarded for council tax.
