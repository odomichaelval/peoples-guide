New Style Jobseeker's Allowance (JSA) is a payment for people who are unemployed or working fewer hours and are looking for work. It is based on the National Insurance contributions you paid in the last two to three tax years, not on your household income or savings.

You may be eligible if you have paid enough Class 1 National Insurance contributions, are under State Pension age, are not in full-time education, and are available for and actively looking for work.

Your partner's income and your savings do not affect New Style JSA. You can get New Style JSA at the same time as Universal Credit; the JSA payment is taken into account when your Universal Credit is worked out.

You apply online. You will have an interview with a work coach at your local Jobcentre and agree a Claimant Commitment describing the steps you will take to find work.
