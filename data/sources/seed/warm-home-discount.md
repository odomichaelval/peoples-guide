The Warm Home Discount is a one-off discount on your electricity bill in winter. Eligibility depends on getting certain benefits (such as the guarantee element of Pension Credit, or some means-tested benefits like Universal Credit) and on your energy supplier taking part in the scheme. Many eligible people get it automatically.

If you are struggling to pay energy bills, contact your energy supplier - they must work with you on an affordable payment plan. Ask about any hardship funds your supplier offers. Citizens Advice can help if you are in energy debt.
