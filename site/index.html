About People’s Guide

People’s Guide brings together starting points for UK residents. Our local housing guidance focuses on Sheffield.

Our mission

Help residents find reliable public service information and understand how to access support.

Our vision

A future where everyone can find and access essential support, however familiar they are with the system.

Sheffield City Council

Housing and rent

Find a starting point if you’re behind on rent or worried about losing your home.

What can I do about rent or housing?

If you might lose your home, contact Sheffield City Council’s Housing Solutions team to discuss your options.

Have your tenancy details and any letters about rent arrears or eviction to hand.

GOV.UK

Job loss and income

Find out what to check if your income has dropped.

What should I check for Universal Credit?

Universal Credit may help with living costs if you’re out of work or on a low income.

Gather information about your income, savings, household and housing costs before checking eligibility.

GOV.UK

Living costs

Get ready to check what financial help you might receive.

What information should I gather?

Make a list of household income, savings, existing benefits, rent and childcare costs.

A benefits calculator can use those details to estimate what support you might receive.
